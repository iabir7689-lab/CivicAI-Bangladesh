# FutureMakers Submission Notes

## Project title
CivicAI Bangladesh — AI-Powered Community Problem Intelligence & Response Platform

## One-line pitch
CivicAI transforms citizen reports into explainable, prioritized civic intelligence so communities and service organizations can respond faster.

## Problem
Civic issues are often reported in fragmented ways. Decision-makers may lack a unified view of issue type, severity, location and concentration.

## Solution
A privacy-aware platform that accepts citizen reports, classifies the issue, estimates severity, calculates a transparent priority score and presents a real-time dashboard.

## 3-minute demo script

### 0:00–0:30 — Problem
Show several fictional reports arriving from different districts.

### 0:30–1:10 — Citizen report
Submit a waterlogging report from the app.

### 1:10–1:50 — AI
Show category, severity, confidence and priority score.

### 1:50–2:30 — Dashboard
Show national queue, district distribution and active incidents.

### 2:30–3:00 — Impact & scale
Explain how the prototype can evolve from campus/city pilots to district-level deployments with stronger NLP, computer vision, secure APIs and human verification.

## What not to claim
Do not claim the prototype is already connected to government systems, uses live nationwide government data, or is 100% bug-free. Present those as future integration goals.
